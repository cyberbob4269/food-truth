# Food Truth

**UK supermarket food intelligence** — quick, research-backed buy/avoid verdicts on everyday products, additives, and misleading labels.

**Live app:** [https://cyberbob4269.github.io/food-truth/](https://cyberbob4269.github.io/food-truth/)

## What it is

Food Truth helps UK shoppers see what is worth buying, what to avoid, and why — with clearer alternatives in mainstream supermarkets. The tone is direct and consumer-facing.
